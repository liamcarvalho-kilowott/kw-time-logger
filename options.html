KW Time Logger

Settings and tokens stay in this browser.

👋Start here

This extension logs your hours to Zoho Projects, so you never have to open Zoho to fill in a timesheet. Setup takes about 5 minutes and you only do it once.

You'll need:

  • Your Zoho login: the same email and password you use for Zoho Projects.
  • This page and a second browser tab for Zoho. No coding and no installs.

Why the extra steps? Zoho doesn’t let a program log in with just your password. Instead, you create a small private “key” in your own Zoho account (steps A–F below) and give it to this extension. Nobody else gets it, and you can delete it in Zoho whenever you like.

2Connect your Zoho account ✓ Done

Finish step 1 first. Then click Connect Zoho and a small Zoho window will pop up.

  • Sign in if it asks.
  • Zoho shows a screen saying KW Time Logger wants access. Click Accept. It asks only to read your projects, tasks and time logs, and to add time logs. It can't delete anything or change your tasks.
  • The window closes by itself and the box below turns green.
Not connected

Your Zoho account belongs to more than one. Pick the one your projects are in.

3When should your hours go to Zoho?

When you add an entry, it waits in a queue. At the time below each day, the queue is handled for you. If your computer is asleep or Chrome is closed then, it happens the next time Chrome opens. Each entry keeps the date you gave it.

Every day at this time you get a (funny) notification, even if nothing is queued. Nothing showing up? In Windows, open Settings → System → Notifications and make sure Google Chrome is on and Focus assist is off.

You can also skip the queue any time with Log now in the popup.

4Smarter typing Optional

You can skip this step. The extension works fine without it.

The ⚡ Type it box lets you write something like “2h on the checkout bug and 30m team meeting yesterday”. Without anything here, it matches your words to your tasks on your own computer, which works for simple sentences. With a Gemini key, Gemini reads the sentence, picks the right tasks, splits it into separate entries and writes a tidy comment.

Good to know: each use sends your sentence and the names of your open tasks to Google. The free tier is usually enough, but Google may use free-tier data to improve its products. If you're at a company, ask whoever manages this whether that's okay.
How do I get a key?
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it. It starts with AIza.
  3. Paste it in the box above. Keep it private.

5You're set. Here's the daily routine

  1. Pin the extension. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to KW Time Logger. The ⚡ icon now stays in your toolbar.
  2. Open it with the ⚡ icon, or press ⌘ Shift L on a Mac (Ctrl Shift L on Windows).
  3. Log time in either of two ways:
    • Type it: write what you did and press ⌘ Enter. It fills the form for you. Check it, then click Add to queue.
    • Use the form: pick a task, enter the hours (like 1:30 or 1.5) and a comment.
  4. Done. The queue goes to Zoho at your end-of-day time. In a hurry? Click Log now instead.

There's also a Todo tab for your own to-do list. A ⏱ Log button on any todo turns it into a time entry.

🛟Something went wrong?

Find your problem below. Most are fixed in a minute.

Zoho says “Invalid redirect URI” or “redirect_uri mismatch”

The redirect address in your Zoho client doesn't match the one in step D.

  1. Go back to the Zoho API Console, open your KW Time Logger client and edit it.
  2. Replace the Authorized Redirect URI with a fresh copy from step D. No spaces at the start or end.
  3. Save, then click Connect Zoho again.

This address changes if the extension is removed and loaded again from a different folder. If that happened, update it in Zoho the same way.

“invalid_client” or “Zoho sign-in failed”

Usually the Client ID or Secret was pasted with a missing character or an extra space, or the wrong region is picked in step A. Re-copy both from Zoho's Client Secret tab, check the region, and try again.

I can’t find “Add Client” in the API Console
  • Check you opened the console for your region (step A). The button on step B uses the region you picked.
  • Make sure you're signed in with your work Zoho account.
  • At some companies only admins can create API clients. If you see an error or no button, ask your Zoho admin to do step 1 for you, or to give you access.
It connected, but I see no tasks
  • Only tasks that are open and assigned to you show up. Ask someone to assign you one, or check it in Zoho.
  • Click Refresh tasks in the popup. Tasks are remembered for 30 minutes.
  • If you have several portals, choose the right one in step 2.
  • Still empty? Open Advanced at the bottom of this page and see the note there.
My hours didn't show up in Zoho
  • Open the popup's Queue tab. If the entry is still there, it hasn't been sent yet. Click Send now.
  • A red message under an entry says why Zoho refused it. Common reasons: the task was closed, the date is locked, or you don't have permission to log time on that project.
  • Check the Sent tab. If it's listed there, Zoho accepted it. In Zoho, open the task's Time or Timesheet area to find it.
The popup asks me to connect again

Your connection was cancelled or expired (for example, if the key was removed in Zoho). Click Connect Zoho in step 2 again. Your queue and todos are kept.

I want to start over or disconnect

Click Disconnect in step 2. This signs you out and cancels the extension's access on Zoho's side. Your queued entries and todos stay where they are.

Advanced

Only needed if your tasks don't show up even though they're assigned to you. The extension normally finds this by itself the first time it sees one of your tasks. Leave it empty unless someone from your team asks you to fill it in.
